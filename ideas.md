**Design Movement:** Dark Cosmos / Premium Developer Tool
**Core Principles:**
- Deep dark navy base (#07071a) with subtle star-field or dot-grid texture
- Instagram gradient (purple → pink → orange) used as the single accent system
- Generous whitespace — content breathes, nothing is crowded
- Card-based product grid with glass-morphism effect

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: deep navy #07071a
- Surface: slightly lighter #0f0f2a with 1px border at 10% white
- Accent: Instagram gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #833ab4, #c13584, #e1306c, #f77737)`
- Text: pure white for headings, #a0a0c0 for body, gradient for highlights

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Centered hero with large display type, icon row, and CTA
- Asymmetric product cards below — left-aligned text, right-aligned icon
- Sticky top nav with glass blur

**Signature Elements:**
- Gradient glow orbs behind hero text (soft radial blur)
- Product cards with gradient border-image on hover
- Thin gradient line divider between sections

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Cards lift on hover with subtle shadow + border glow
- Nav items underline with gradient on hover
- CTA button pulses gently

**Animation:**
- Fade-up entrance for hero text (staggered)
- Card hover: translateY(-4px) + gradient border reveal
- Gradient orb slowly drifts in background

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Sora" (bold 700/800) for headlines
- Body: "DM Sans" (400/500) for descriptions
- Mono accent: "JetBrains Mono" for version badges
</text>
